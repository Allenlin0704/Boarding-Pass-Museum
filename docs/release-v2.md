# BoardingPassMuseum V2 更新日志

> 发布日期：正式发布时填写

## 简体中文

### BoardingPassMuseum V2｜让每一张票，都有自己的位置

V1 带来了线上展厅和投稿入口。V2 把收藏做得更完整：登机牌、火车票和地铁票可以放在同一个展厅里，也能按航班、线路和出行信息重新找到彼此。

**票种与投稿更完整**

- 新增火车票和地铁票投稿，可选择运营公司并填写车次、线路及国家或地区信息。
- 登机牌可填写航线范围、舱位和行李标记；国内航线会自动隐藏不需要的到达国家或地区字段。
- 图片处理器支持旋转、裁剪、隐私遮挡和压缩。图片会统一处理后再上传；投稿前可以预览并检查遮挡。
- 票面识别可协助填写信息，结果仍需投稿者核对。

**展品之间可以相遇**

- 展厅增加多语言筛选与排序，航司和机场中英文名称保持关联。
- 星图按航班号合并节点；展品详情会推荐相同航班或出发地点的其他收藏。
- 展品故事支持按需翻译，翻译结果会保存，之后再次查看可直接使用。
- 分享卡可以带上展品图片与链接，方便分享一次真实旅程。

**个人收藏有了成长记录**

- 个人主页展示等级、等级进度、累计投稿和成就；管理员还可查看经手审核数量。
- 点开成就可以查看获得条件和自己的达成原因。
- 社区动态与个人展品在主页并排展示，手机上会按纵向顺序排列。

**审核和社区管理更清楚**

- 投稿状态统一为待筛查、审核中、已通过或已拒绝，并提供对应的处理入口。
- 普通管理员处理分配给自己的稿件；站主可处理全部投稿、申诉与社区管理事项。
- 用户可以恢复自己下架的展品，让它重新进入审核队列。
- 社区支持发帖、评论、点赞、举报和违规处理，并保留公告记录供之后查看。

**中英文与繁体中文同步完善**

主要页面、投稿流程、个人主页和管理界面补齐英文与繁体中文显示。网站继续适配手机和桌面浏览器。

谢谢每一位把旅程留在这里的收藏者。愿这些小小的票根，帮我们记住走过的地方，也遇见同行的人。

## English

### BoardingPassMuseum V2 | Every ticket has a place

V1 introduced the online gallery and its submission flow. V2 brings more of each journey together: boarding passes, train tickets, and metro tickets can live in one collection and connect through their flights, routes, and travel details.

**More ways to collect**

- Submit train and metro tickets with their operator, service or line, and country or region.
- Add route scope, cabin class, and baggage tags to boarding passes. The arrival country or region field is hidden for domestic routes.
- Rotate, crop, redact, and compress images in the built-in editor. Preview the result and check privacy masks before submitting.
- Ticket recognition can help fill in details; contributors should review every result.

**Exhibits can find one another**

- Explore with multilingual filters and sorting, with linked Chinese and English names for airlines and airports.
- The flight map keeps one node per flight number. Exhibit pages can point to other collections with the same flight or departure point.
- Translate exhibit stories on demand. Translations are saved so they can be reused on later visits.
- Share cards include an exhibit image and a link to the collection.

**A collection that grows with you**

- Profiles show level, progress, total submissions, and achievements. Administrators can also see their review count.
- Open an achievement to see how it is earned and why it was awarded to you.
- Community posts and personal exhibits share the profile page, arranged in a single column on phones.

**Clearer review and community tools**

- Submission states distinguish screening, review, approval, and rejection, with a clear next step for each.
- Administrators review assigned submissions; the site owner can handle all submissions, appeals, and community moderation.
- Contributors can restore their own hidden exhibits and send them back for review.
- The community supports posts, comments, likes, reports, moderation, and an announcement archive.

**English and Traditional Chinese**

English and Traditional Chinese have been added across key pages, submission steps, profiles, and management screens. The site continues to support both phones and desktop browsers.

Thank you to everyone who has shared a journey here. Each small ticket stub helps us remember where we have been—and find others who love the journey too.
